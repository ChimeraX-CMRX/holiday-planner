Self-hosted travel planning

One holiday.
Every place, journey and plan.

A lightweight web app for building multi-city trips with maps, saved places, travel legs and a morning-to-night calendar.

Map every stopSeparate destination tabs, familiar map pins and cached OpenStreetMap tiles.
Plan the whole dayAssign multiple places or notes to morning, afternoon, evening and night.
Join the journeyTrack flights, trains, ferries and other travel as planned or booked.
Keep it yoursYour plans live in a local SQLite database that is never part of the repository.

Fictional two-week demo

European Rail Adventure

Amsterdam, Paris, Zürich and Rome, with famous landmarks, restaurants, fictional flights and rail journeys. The demo contains no real traveller or booking data.

Simple deployment

Choose your own address and port.

HOLIDAY_HOST=0.0.0.0 \
HOLIDAY_PORT=8085 \
python3 server.py

Python 3.9+ is the only runtime dependency. A hardened systemd service, environment template and one-command demo seed are included.
